You must be at least 18 years old, have a high school diploma or GED, be able to pass a background check and drug screening, and meet basic physical requirements set by the airlines. No prior experience is needed.

No aviation or flight attendant experience is required. Our program is designed for people who are new to the industry and want a structured, supported path into an airline career.
