Who We Are

A Little About Us

Est. 1972

Welcome to the
Airline Academy

Established in 1972, we have become one of the finest aviation training facilities in the world.

Students from over 63 countries, in addition to all fifty states, have attended our programs. In 1996, customer service and flight attendant training were added to the curriculum to meet the ongoing personnel needs of the commercial airline industry. Today, our airline customer service and flight attendant students receive hands-on training at the multi-million-dollar JetBlue University in Orlando, Florida, including emergency evacuation training in a real airline training environment and access to a full-motion simulator that meets FAR 121 standards.

Our placement team includes current flight attendants, former flight attendants, and a former airline recruiter. Each graduating class is afforded the opportunity to interview with various carriers upon graduation under the guidance of our staff. The Airline Academy holds a letter of exemption from the State of Florida Board of Education through its contract training partnerships with FAA Part 121-certified air carriers.

Mission

Fast-track training for a successful airline career

We offer a low-cost, short-term program designed to maximize your education while minimizing time away from home and in the classroom. Our focused curriculum equips you with the essential skills needed to gain a competitive edge and succeed in the airline industry.

History

A legacy of excellence in aviation

Founder Nino Caravita retired as US Airways' all-time senior pilot, logging over 40,000 flight hours. His leadership extended beyond the cockpit, serving as Vice President of the Airline Pilots Association (ALPA), Chairman of the Aviation Safety Board, and a member of President Richard Nixon's Aviation Advisory Board.

Instructors

Experienced professionals
guiding your success.

The Airline Academy instructors are carefully selected for their extensive industry experience with major carriers such as Delta Airlines, American Airlines, and JetBlue. With a student-focused approach, they provide the most comprehensive training available, ensuring quality education with a maximum 10:1 student-teacher ratio.

Flight attendant students train at the state-of-the-art JetBlue University in Orlando, Florida, a multi-million-dollar facility. This includes access to a full-motion simulator that meets FAR 121 standards, providing hands-on experience in a realistic aviation environment.
